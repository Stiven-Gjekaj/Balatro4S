# Roadmap

Each release adds one big part. The risky work comes first: the renderer,
the assets, and the speed on the A5. The reason for each choice is in
[docs/decisions.md](docs/decisions.md).

## 0.1: the foundation

- Copy the scripts, the packaging, and the test runner from Groovelet, with
  the prefix `BO`.
- Read the textures and the font from `Balatro.love` on the phone.
- Draw sprites from the atlases of Balatro with OpenGL ES 2.0, in landscape,
  at 60 frames per second.
- Deal a hand of 8 cards. Select up to 5 cards with a tap.
- Find the poker hand, and score it with the base chips and mult.

## 0.2: a full run with the standard deck

- Play and discard. Small, Big, and Boss blinds. Cash out.
- Antes 1 to 8, the win screen, and the game over screen.
- The seeded random generator.

## 0.3: the shop and the first jokers

- The shop, buy, sell, reroll, money, and interest.
- The joker engine, and the first 40 jokers.

## 0.4: consumables and packs

- Tarot, Planet, and Spectral cards. Booster packs.
- Hand levels.

## 0.5: card modifiers, vouchers, tags, and all blinds

- Enhancements, editions, and seals.
- All vouchers, all tags, and all boss blinds.

## 0.7: the feel

## 0.8: progress
